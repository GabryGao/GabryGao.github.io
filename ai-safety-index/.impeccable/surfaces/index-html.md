Scope: site home page (index.html) and the pages sharing its shell, Operate mode, code-led. Audience: researchers and people following AI safety who want to know what happened this week. Data: news-feed.js (AI Safety HOT records, 7-day window), no invented scores or grades.

## Direction contract

THESIS: AI safety as a live "days since last incident" works board, not a resource directory. Refuses the white card grid + search box + six-directions layout.
OWN-WORLD: industrial safety signage (ISO 7010 safe-condition green field, white enamel number plates, warning yellow only on incident days, signal red only on high-heat incidents) on a graphite ground; Archivo condensed heavy for numerals and headings, Archivo normal for UI, system CJK sans.
STORY: visitor sees "N days since the last AI safety incident" and the 7-day incident strip, understands incidents happen almost daily, scans which organisations generated safety signals this week, clicks through to original sources.
FIRST VIEWPORT: standard top nav; left 7/12 green board with flip-plate day counter, last incident headline/source/date, 7-day strip; right 5/12 last-72h signal list ranked by heat. Organisation scorecard table header starts below the fold line.
FORM: works safety board as operate dashboard, user-pinned direction (A+D). seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
