# AI Safety Index

A public, buildless static portal connecting AI safety research directions, papers, data/tasks, benchmarks, published results and source-linked research news. It serves researchers, developers and people following AI safety. Its primary job is practical: help visitors quickly find usable resources. Source-linked editorial recommendations and community research promotion complement discovery; neither a ranking nor a recommendation implies endorsement of research claims. Canonical hosting is https://gabrygao.github.io/ai-safety-index/ in `GabryGao/GabryGao.github.io`, under `ai-safety-index/`. Preserve other repository files during publication.

## Evidence and source policy

The library contains 490 bibliography records, including 487 selected from OpenTAI's public GitHub catalog at commit `8910c895c22f90f78205ad45ae0efb3461e63225`, plus three existing records. Titles, authors, dates and original links retain attribution in `research-source/opentai-import-provenance.json`. The cached official metadata title match is not independent full-text review or experimental reproduction. Do not imply that inclusion establishes a paper's claims. Nine historical HELM Safety report records retain model versions and evaluation conditions; they are not a current leaderboard. `research-source/helm-safety-results-provenance.json` records the 2024-11-08 Stanford CRFM source and exact two-setting values. The results view shares model/developer/configuration filters between its chart, table and CSV export. Original 0–1 values are multiplied by 100 for display, not reinterpreted as an attack/refusal rate. The full results dashboard uses horizontal bars on every viewport; detailed tables scroll within their own region. Home draws its two charts from the same three of these nine historical records: a vertical bar chart switches between default and GCG-T settings, and a horizontal dumbbell chart compares both settings for the same model versions with an expandable numeric table. In source-record order, the displayed default values are 98.1, 84.9 and 82.9, and GCG-T values are 87.9, 76.5 and 62.2. Both charts identify HELM Safety v1.0, HarmBench, the 2024-11-08 report, three-of-nine scope and source ×100 scaling; they are neither a current ranking nor a measure of overall safety. Preserve original-source links and numeric result tables.

Research news combines manually selected/archived records with up to 30 keyword-matched metadata entries from the public OpenAI and Google DeepMind RSS feeds. Automated entries are visibly unreviewed, retain publisher dates and original titles, and use provisional topic labels. Their two-publisher coverage is not comprehensive. `research-source/news-feed-provenance.json` records collection time, source health and provenance. No article bodies, copied abstracts or paid API are used. A failed source retains its previous metadata.

`tools/update-news.py` updates the generated feed and has 17 ingestion tests. A daily repository workflow is prepared separately for publication; this document does not establish that it has been installed or successfully run. Runtime fetching of the public raw GitHub JSON validates record structure, known topics, publisher hosts, dates and automated/unreviewed flags before accepting newer data. Bundled, dated metadata remains the offline fallback. An open news view offers an explicit refresh; background updates do not rerender drafts or reset filters.
